About Indigo Pulse

Studio jazz shaped by rhythm, atmosphere, and melodic restraint.

Indigo Pulse is a studio jazz project exploring new rhythmic shapes, modal harmony, and atmospheric ensemble textures without leaving melody behind. The music often moves through odd meters and asymmetrical grooves, but the intent is not technical display. It is about making time feel wider, stranger, and more emotionally charged.

Rooted in contemporary modal jazz, Indigo Pulse draws on shifting pulse, spacious arrangement, and late-night harmonic color. The project sits between familiar jazz language and less predictable rhythmic terrain: accessible enough to invite the listener in, detailed enough to reward close attention.
